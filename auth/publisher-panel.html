📖 How to Get a Google Drive Link

▼
  1. Go to drive.google.com and upload your file.
  2. Right-click the file and select "Share" (or click the share icon 🔗).
  3. Under "General access", click the dropdown and change it from Restricted to "Anyone with the link".
  4. Make sure the role is set to "Viewer".
  5. Click "Copy link" — the link will look like:
    https://drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID/view?usp=sharing
  6. Paste it in the "Google Drive Shareable Link" field below — it will be automatically converted to a direct download link.
✓ Auto-conversion
Shareable link → https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=FILE_ID
